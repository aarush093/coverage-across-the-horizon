## LIT_2604.13253_bc-aci
- **Metadata:** Ankit Lade, Sai Krishna J., Indar Kumar (latter two equal contribution). "Bias-Corrected Adaptive Conformal Inference for Multi-Horizon Time Series Forecasting". arXiv:2604.13253v1 [cs.LG], cross-listed stat.ME / stat.ML, submitted 14 Apr 2026. 14 pages, 3 figures, 2 tables. **Preprint — no venue.** Verified? **Y, 2026-08-30** (abstract page + HTML v1 + stat.ME listing all resolve).
- **Research problem:** ACI adapts only the quantile threshold α_t; it cannot move the interval *centre*. When a deployed forecaster develops persistent bias after a distribution shift — the realistic case of a model trained once and not retrained for weeks — ACI must widen symmetrically to cover an offset, paying a width overhead the authors put at ≈2|b| for bias magnitude b.
- **Method (one paragraph):** BC-ACI adds per-horizon online exponentially-weighted moving-average estimation of forecast bias, subtracts that estimate from the nonconformity scores *before* the quantile is computed, and re-centres the resulting interval. The claim is that this addresses the cause of miscalibration (a shifted residual mean) rather than the symptom (an under-covering symmetric band). It is a lightweight online extension, not a retrained model.
- **Datasets:** Motivating example is a ridge regression on 500 synthetic steps with a level shift of δ=5 (post-shift residual mean b≈3.99, σ≈1.03; ACI reaches 90% coverage at average width 8.67).
