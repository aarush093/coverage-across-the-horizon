## LIT_2601.18509_cp-ts-benchmark
- **Metadata:** Andro Sabashvili (single author). "Conformal Prediction Algorithms for Time Series Forecasting: Methods and Benchmarking". arXiv:2601.18509 [cs.LG]. Submitted 26 Jan 2026, v2 30 Jan 2026. Source 25 KB. **Preprint — no venue.** Verified? **Y, 2026-08-30** (abstract page fetched in full).
- **Research problem:** conformal prediction assumes exchangeability, which time series violates. The paper surveys and benchmarks the four families that respond to this: methods relaxing exchangeability, methods redefining the data unit as a collection of independent series, methods modelling residual dynamics, and online learning methods that adapt to shift for long-run coverage.
- **Method:** a survey plus one empirical benchmark. **Base forecaster: AutoARIMA. Data: a large-scale monthly sales dataset.**
- **Metrics:** marginal coverage, interval width, Winkler score. **No conditional coverage. No per-channel or per-horizon breakdown. No worst-cell.**
- **Headline result:** multi-step split conformal prediction (MSCP) meets the 90% coverage threshold and is the most efficient method benchmarked.
- **Strengths:** the cleanest available taxonomy of the CP-for-time-series literature, and it formalises MSCP as a named baseline paradigm — which is what fix F1 in the Idea Lock already assumed.
- **Weaknesses:** single author, single base forecaster, single dataset family, marginal metrics only. AutoARIMA on monthly sales is a different regime from 96–720-step LTSF on multivariate benchmarks.
- **Relevance to OUR wedge — this resolves Q05, and it resolves it in our favour.**
  - **Datasets:** monthly sales. Ours: ETT ×4 and LTSF Electricity. **No overlap.**
  - **Backbones:** AutoARIMA. Ours: DLinear, NLinear (frozen LTSF point forecasters). **No overlap.**
  - **Metrics:** marginal coverage, width, Winkler. Ours adds conditional coverage over a horizon-bucket × channel grid, worst-cell, and the D009 distributional statistics. **This is precisely the axis W1 claims.**
- **Collision verdict: NONE. Q05 CLOSED — it does not pre-empt W1.**
- **The positioning point, and it is the strongest one available.** This is the closest benchmarking work in the literature, and its conclusion is that **MSCP is the best method**. On our surface MSCP has the **highest marginal coverage of all seven methods (0.9123)** and the **worst conditional coverage of all seven (worst-cell 0.7251, below the do-nothing Global baseline at 0.7667)**.
